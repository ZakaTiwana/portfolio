LAAF - Flutter APP.

LAAF is  a hybrid application written on Flutter for Android and IOS. 
LAAF has two types of users, namely ‘Users’ and ‘Caregivers’.
Users are be able to register and login into LAAF. They are be able to 
view and update their profile. They are be able to connect to their ‘Insole’ and 
are be able to see the data extracted from them in ‘My Feet’. 
They are also be able to view exercise. They are be able to view, like, bookmark 
and share blogs. They are also be able to find Caregivers, see their details, 
get in contact with them and request appointments with them. 
They are also be able to see their upcoming appointments and their appointments history.
They are also be able to rate their appointments, 
products and LAAF app. They are be able to visit LAAF stores to buy LAAF products.
Caregivers are be able to register and login into LAAF. 
They are be able to view and update their profile.
They are be able to add and view their patients (i.e., Users) and see their history.
They are be able to create and view appointments with their patients. 
They are be able to view, like, bookmark and share blogs. 
They are be able to visit LAAF stores to view and buy LAAF products.
And They are able to chat with one another.
